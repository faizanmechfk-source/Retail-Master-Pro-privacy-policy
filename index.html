1. Introduction & Developer Identity

Welcome to Retail Master Pro ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), an enterprise point-of-sale (POS), billing, inventory management, and digital customer khata (ledger) application developed for retail merchants, shop owners, and commercial enterprises.

This Privacy Policy outlines how Retail Master Pro accesses, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application (Package ID: com.retailmaster.pro). By installing, accessing, or using the Application, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this document.

If you have any questions or require clarification regarding this policy, you may contact our dedicated developer and data protection administrator at faizan.rakhan@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect & Store

Retail Master Pro operates primarily on an Offline-First Local Architecture. Data entered into the application resides directly within an encrypted local Room/SQLite database on your mobile device. The types of data processed include:

A. Store & Business Profile Information

  • Business Identity: Store name, registered business name, store logo or image, tagline.
  • Contact Details: Store phone number, business email address, physical storefront address.
  • Tax & Compliance Data: GSTIN / Tax Identification Number, state code, PAN (if entered), invoice terms and conditions.
  • Preferences: Currency symbol (e.g., ₹, $, €, AED), default tax rates, thermal printer paper size preferences (58mm / 80mm).

B. Inventory, Stock & Catalog Data

  • Item name, description, brand, category, SKU, barcode/EAN number.
  • Wholesale/Purchase price, retail selling price, Maximum Retail Price (MRP).
  • Stock quantities, units of measurement (Pcs, Kg, Ltr, Box), minimum reorder alert levels.

C. Customer & Supplier Ledgers (Khata / Udhar)

  • Customer & supplier names, contact phone numbers, billing/delivery addresses.
  • Debit and credit transaction records, payment due dates, partial payments, payment remarks.
  • Balance due summaries and transaction timestamps.

D. Sales, Invoices & Financial Reports

  • Order IDs, invoice numbers, transaction dates and timestamps.
  • Sold items list, item quantities, discounts applied, taxes (CGST/SGST/IGST/VAT).
  • Payment mode recorded (Cash, UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or Credit/Udhar).
  • Expense tracking records (rent, salaries, utility bills, supplier payouts).

3. Device Permissions Explained

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, Retail Master Pro requests only the minimal set of Android system permissions strictly necessary to fulfill its core inventory and billing functionality.

android.permission.CAMERA Runtime Permission

Purpose: Used exclusively to activate your device camera for real-time barcode and QR code scanning of products. This allows lightning-fast product lookups and cart creation during billing.
Privacy Safeguard: The camera feed is analyzed in real time on the device. We do NOT record video, take unsolicited photographs, or transmit camera footage to any remote server.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime Permission (Android 13+)

Purpose: Used to display critical operational alerts directly to you, such as low-stock warnings when inventory is depleted, overdue customer credit payment alerts, and scheduled database backup reminders.
Privacy Safeguard: We do not use push notifications for unsolicited advertising, promotional spam, or third-party marketing. You can revoke this permission anytime in Android System Settings.

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Install-Time Permission

Purpose: Used to check network connectivity status and perform secure cloud backups (when cloud sync is enabled by you), verify license validity, download official app updates, and sync authenticated account state.

android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Normal Install-Time Permission

Purpose: Enables the app to detect active Wi-Fi connections to allow local thermal POS receipt printing over network printers and optimize large cloud backups.

androidx.core.content.FileProvider Scoped Document Sharing

Purpose: Allows the application to generate and export professional PDF invoices, Excel spreadsheets, and GST tax reports so you can share them with your customers or accountants via WhatsApp, Email, or Bluetooth printers without requesting legacy broad storage permissions.

4. How We Use Your Information

Any information processed by Retail Master Pro is used strictly for legitimate commercial and application management purposes:

  • To generate digital tax invoices, retail cash memos, and thermal receipts with your custom business branding.
  • To calculate accurate gross sales, profit margins, product turnover rates, and GST/VAT tax liabilities.
  • To manage customer credit (Udhar Khata) and generate automated digital payment reminders.
  • To monitor product stock levels and notify you before high-demand inventory sells out.
  • To facilitate secure database backup and disaster recovery on user demand.
  • To diagnose application crashes and performance bottlenecks to improve system stability.

We DO NOT:

  • Sell, monetize, rent, or trade your inventory data, customer phone numbers, or sales figures.
  • Track your online browsing behavior across external websites or applications.
  • Display personalized third-party advertisements within the core billing workflow.

5. Data Storage & Security Protocols

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational security measures to protect your sensitive business records:

Storage Layer Location Protection Mechanism
Primary Database Local Device Sandbox SQLite / Room Database secured inside application-private sandboxed internal storage; inaccessible to unauthorized apps.
Cloud Backup (Optional) Google Cloud / Firebase End-to-end transport security using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption in transit; encrypted at rest via AES-256.
Invoice & Report Files Android Cache & App Provider Scoped storage via Android FileProvider, temporary file leases granted strictly during user-initiated share intents.

9. User Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & India DPDPA)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you hold specific statutory rights regarding your personal and business data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can export all inventory, sales transactions, and khata records anytime into open, readable PDF and Excel formats.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit and update any incorrect product, customer, or business record directly in the app.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the absolute right to delete your data locally and request deletion of any cloud backups.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can revoke runtime permissions (Camera, Notifications) at any time through Android Settings without bricking the core offline manual billing functionality.
